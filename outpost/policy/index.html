Outpost: Zero Day

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Renat Musaev (“I”, “me”, or “we”) handles information in the mobile game Outpost: Zero Day (the “App”), package ID studio.veex.outpost.

Overview

Outpost: Zero Day is a server-authoritative game. To play, the App creates a player account and syncs game progress with our game servers. You can optionally link Apple or Google sign-in, watch rewarded ads, and enable push notifications.

Information we process

Account and authentication. When you start the App, we create a guest account with an internal user ID. Authentication tokens are stored on your device so you can stay signed in. If you link Sign in with Apple or Google, we verify the identity token with that provider and may store the email address the provider shares with us, together with the provider account link needed to sign you back in.

Game progress. We store your server-side game state on our servers, including resources, base and crafting progress, equipment, inventory, expedition and map progress, economy balances, and related gameplay records. The App syncs this state while you play.

Device and local data. On your device the App may store auth tokens (in secure storage), settings preferences, cached game media (such as images), and a limited local diagnostics log used to troubleshoot crashes and connection problems. Diagnostics are designed not to include passwords, tokens, or request bodies.

Analytics. The App uses Firebase Analytics (Google) to measure installs, app opens, and gameplay or technical events that help us operate and improve the App. Firebase may process device and app identifiers and usage data as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy information.

Push notifications. If you allow notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging. Google may process a device push token and related delivery data. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in device settings or in the App settings where available.

Advertising. The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for optional rewarded ads (for example energy or coin rewards). Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver and measure ads. See Google’s Privacy Policy. You can limit personalized advertising in your iOS or Android privacy settings.

Server logs. Our servers keep limited operational logs such as request method, path, status code, timing, and authenticated user ID. We do not intentionally log request headers, query strings, or payloads in those diagnostics.

Support. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

How information is used

  • To create and maintain your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run the game, sync progress, and prevent cheating, abuse, and technical problems.
  • To measure App performance and improve features and stability.
  • To deliver optional rewarded ads and measure ad completion for in-game rewards.
  • To send optional push notifications you have allowed.
  • To answer support, privacy, and account requests.
  • To comply with law and store policies where required.

Legal bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

Where required, we process information to perform the contract of providing the App (account and game progress), with your consent where you opt into features such as personalized ads or notifications, and for legitimate interests in securing, operating, and improving the App, balanced against your rights.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with service providers only as needed to operate the App:

  • Google / Firebase — analytics, push messaging, and AdMob advertising.
  • Apple / Google — identity verification when you choose Sign in with Apple or Google.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that process server data on our behalf.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a transfer of the App’s operations.

Retention

Account and game progress are kept while your account remains active. If you delete a linked account in the App, we delete the account and associated server progress. Operational logs are kept only as long as needed for security and diagnostics. Local device data is removed when you clear App data or uninstall, subject to any platform backups you control.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a linked account. If your account is linked with Apple or Google, use Delete account in the App (More / Account). This permanently deletes the account and server progress.
  • Guest sessions. A guest account is a device session. Uninstalling or clearing App data removes local access tokens. To request deletion of guest server data, email support@veex.studio and include your device platform, App version, and any account or player ID shown in the App if available. You can also link Apple or Google and then use Delete account.
  • Advertising. Rewarded ads are optional. You may limit ad personalization in system settings (for example App Tracking Transparency / Limit Ad Tracking on iOS, or Google advertising settings on Android).
  • Notifications. You may disable push notifications in device settings or in the App settings.
  • Access and correction. Email support@veex.studio for privacy requests, including access, correction, or deletion where applicable law provides those rights.

Children

Outpost: Zero Day is not directed to children under 13, or under the applicable minimum age in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

International transfers

We and our service providers (including Google) may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for such transfers as described by those providers.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including authenticated API access and secure on-device token storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests: support@veex.studio.
