Help with Outpost: Zero Day

Email support@veex.studio if you need help.

Please include:

  • Your device model and operating system version.
  • Your Outpost: Zero Day version.
  • Whether you play as a guest or with Apple / Google linked.
  • A short description of the problem and what happened before it.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if available.

Do not include passwords, payment card details, full auth tokens, or other sensitive information.

Account and progress

Progress is stored on our game servers and synced while you play. Linking Sign in with Apple or Google helps protect progress if you reinstall the App.

To delete a linked Apple or Google account and its server progress, open More → Account → Delete account in the App.

For guest data deletion requests, email support with the details above and any player or account ID shown in the App.

Rewarded ads

If an ad finishes without granting a reward, check your connection, return to the game, and wait a few seconds. Daily reward limits may apply. If the problem continues, restart the App and contact support with the approximate time of the issue.

Notifications

Push notifications are optional. You can enable or disable them in App settings and in your device system settings.

Crashes or startup problems

Restart your device, install the latest App and operating system updates, and make sure storage space and network access are available. If you are still on a guest session, contact support before clearing App data so we can try to identify your progress first.
